HOW TO PLAY

⌨️ Keyboard

  • A / ← Move Left
  • D / → Move Right
  • W / ↑ Accelerate
  • S / ↓ Brake
  • SPACE Jump / Fly
  • SHIFT Boost
  • P / ESC Pause
  • R Restart

📱 Mobile / Touch

  • ← → On-screen buttons to steer
  • ⬆ Jump / Flight button
  • ⚡ Boost button
  • ⏸ Pause button
  • Swipe left/right to change lanes

🏆 Scoring

  • Distance earns base points
  • Collect energy orbs for bonus
  • Near misses give combo points
  • Aerial flips earn trick score
  • Speed boosts your multiplier

⚠️ Obstacles

  • Road barriers — swerve around
  • Futuristic traffic — change lanes
  • Energy gates — fly through
  • Road gaps — jump or fly over
  • Low bridges — duck under
